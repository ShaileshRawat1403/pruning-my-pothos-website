Voice Philosophy · sans_serif_sentiments

Sans Serif Sentiments

My voice philosophy. Three words, each one doing work.

Sansthe absence

The noise. The jargon. The borrowed cleverness. The performance. The unnecessary weight around the idea. Sans is the discipline of absence.

Serifthe excess

The flourish, the extra stroke added after the letter is already readable. Craft matters and texture matters. But when the embellishment starts hiding the thought, it has to go.

Sentimentswhat remains

Not sentimentality, not decorative emotion. The human signal left behind when the noise, excess, and performance are removed. The real observation, the real consequence.

The absence of excess, so the human signal remains.
Two Layers

Where writing, reflection, and critique live. It notices what people miss when they rush toward tools, trends, or execution. A way of thinking in public.

Operating Principles
Clarity should survive complexity.

The one thread through the writing, the systems, and the AI workflows.

AI is an execution layer, not a replacement for thinking.

It accelerates drafting, coding, mapping, and testing. The judgment stays yours.

Build fast. Inspect slowly. Leave a guardrail behind.

Debugging the Vibe: build with AI while keeping system risk visible.

The dependence is here to stay. Build the backup layer.

Stay able to explain and continue the work when the tool fails or changes.

Practical before perfect.

Usable before polished. Perfection too early usually hides the real learning.

Systems before frameworks.

Understand the problem, the workflow, and the risk before reaching for a tool.

Evaluation before confidence.

Good output once is not enough. Find where a system fails before users do.

Documentation is product.

It shapes how people understand, trust, and maintain a system, not just support it.

When everyone can build, clarity becomes the real constraint.

Natural language is the new API, and intent is the interface.
