In ambiguous situations, teams often confuse speed with clarity. The result is a fluent decision that no one can explain later.

A simple decision rule creates stability: allow, ask, deny, or defer based on policy and evidence. It does not remove uncertainty, but it makes uncertainty manageable.

Uncertainty handled explicitly is safer than certainty performed.

**What this changes in practice:** define decision modes before incidents, then log why each mode was chosen.
