---
title: 'Autonomy needs a brake.'
summary: 'Agentic systems become trustworthy when they can pause, verify, and escalate instead of only continuing.'
category: 'Judgment'
tags:

---

Autonomy sounds powerful until the system reaches an uncertain step with real consequences.

That is the moment when a pause, a check, or a human handoff becomes part of the intelligence rather than a weakness in it.

**What this changes in practice:** design agent loops with clear stop conditions, not just clever next-step generation.
