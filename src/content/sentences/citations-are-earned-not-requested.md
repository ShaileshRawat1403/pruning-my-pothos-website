You cannot force a model to cite your page. You can only make your page the easiest reliable evidence to select.

Specific claims, clean structure, and coherent internal linking increase citation confidence. Vague writing creates ambiguity, and ambiguity gets skipped.

Generated visibility is mostly a trust decision made under time pressure.

**What this changes in practice:** publish fewer, sharper pages that can stand as evidence.
