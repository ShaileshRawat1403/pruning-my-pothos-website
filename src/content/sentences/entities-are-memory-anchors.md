A system cannot preserve meaning if it cannot resolve identity. When your terms drift across pages, engines may fragment your ideas into unrelated pieces.

Entity consistency is how your work stays coherent at scale. It tells search systems and answer systems that the same concept is being developed, not replaced.

Naming is not cosmetic. It is memory design.

**What this changes in practice:** keep one stable vocabulary for your core frameworks across all sections.
