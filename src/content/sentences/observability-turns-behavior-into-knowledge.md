A model answer can look correct while the runtime behavior is still wrong. That gap is where trust erodes.

Observability makes that gap visible. When decisions, evidence, and outcomes are linked, teams stop arguing from confidence and start learning from traces.

You do not improve what you cannot replay.

**What this changes in practice:** require step-level evidence for critical workflows before scaling automation.
